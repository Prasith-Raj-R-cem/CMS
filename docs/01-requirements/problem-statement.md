# Problem Statement

<p style="text-align: justify;">
The current academic environment often requires students and faculty to depend on multiple communication channels and manually maintained records for academic information. Assignment deadlines, project submissions, notebook submissions, class timetables, examination schedules, announcements, attendance, and academic results may be distributed across different platforms or communicated individually by faculty members. This makes it difficult for students to keep track of important academic activities and deadlines.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
Faculty members also face difficulties when managing assignments, submission dates, attendance, academic records, and temporary changes to teaching periods. When a faculty member is unavailable, arranging substitute periods or borrowing another faculty member's period is often handled through informal communication, which can lead to confusion and timetable conflicts.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
Administrators need to maintain student and faculty information, class and subject details, class timetables, examination schedules, and other academic information in an organized manner.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
Therefore, there is a need for a centralized Campus Management System that brings these academic activities into a single platform. The system will provide role-based functionality for students, faculty members, and administrators while maintaining academic information in a centralized MySQL database.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
The proposed system will allow students to view their academic timetable, examination timetable, assignment deadlines, calendar events, attendance, results, and notifications. Faculty members will be able to manage assignments, attendance, marks, announcements, and period requests. Administrators will be able to manage users, subjects, classes, timetables, examination schedules, and other academic information.
</p>
